# Purpose

- Solve the camera of a piece of footage, so a 3D model composited over it moves with the plate.

## Ownership

- `src/footage.rs` - `read_footage`: decodes a video's luma through the `ffmpeg` CLI at most `max_width` wide, with its frame rate from `ffprobe`. `Footage::read_matte` decodes a matte video at the frames' size into `Footage::matte`.
- `src/image.rs` - `Gray` frames, Sobel gradients, `Pyramid` levels, and bilinear and Catmull-Rom bicubic sampling.
- `src/features.rs` - Shi-Tomasi corners with a minimum spacing, away from existing tracks, masked pixels and the border.
- `src/klt.rs` - pyramidal Lucas-Kanade (`track_point`) and the affine Gauss-Newton match of a track against its first appearance (`template`, `align`).
- `src/tracks.rs` - `track`: frame-to-frame tracks over a whole shot, kept clear of a matte.
- `src/geometry.rs` - `Intrinsics` (focal length, principal point and radial distortion), normalized eight-point essential matrices and four-point homographies with RANSAC, their factoring into candidate poses, cheirality and Sampson support, triangulation and PnP resection.
- `src/bundle.rs` - Levenberg-Marquardt bundle adjustment with a Schur complement over cameras, points and the lens parameters a `Refine` names (the focal length, the radial distortion k1 and k2).
- `src/solve.rs` - `solve`, `SolveOptions`, `CameraSolve` and `Pose`: the incremental reconstruction and the ground-plane world.
- `src/rotation.rs` - the solve of a camera that only turns: frame-to-frame rotations by two-track RANSAC and Kabsch, refined by alternating the tracks' world directions, every frame's rotation and, for a zoom, every later frame's focal length by least squares, with a golden-section search over frame 0's focal length.
- `src/probe.rs` - `light_probe`, `Probe` and `direction`: an equirectangular light probe stitched from the footage through its solved cameras, and its Radiance `.hdr` encoding.
- `examples/solve.rs` - `cargo run --release -p director-track --example solve -- <footage> [--fov DEG] [--fixed] [--distortion K1 K2] [--height M] [--pitch DEG] [--zoom] [--matte FILE] [--json OUT]` prints the tracking and solve timings, track lengths, the lens and its distortion, the reprojection error and the camera path, and writes the `CameraSolve` as JSON.

## Local Contracts

- The crate links no Skia and no GPU. It depends on `nalgebra`, `rayon` and `serde`, and `read_footage` needs `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` in PATH.
- `track` follows up to 500 corners at least 10 px apart through a 4-level pyramid with bicubic sampling. A step survives a forward-backward round trip within 0.5 px, then an affine match against the track's first 17x17 appearance corrects it, moving it at most 1.5 px with a mean absolute difference below 10, so long tracks do not drift. New corners seed where live ones thinned out. Tracks shorter than 3 frames drop.
- A matte keeps moving things out of the solve: white (luma above half) marks pixels to ignore, frame k reads matte frame k and later frames the last one. `track` dilates each matte frame by 8 px, the reach of the 17x17 template, seeds no corner there and ends a track that enters it. `solve` fails when the matte's size differs from the frames'. An empty matte leaves out nothing. Movers that slide along the epipolar lines of the camera's travel, such as a car driving parallel to a tracking shot, fit the epipolar geometry and the bundle almost as well as the static scene, so the solve does not detect them on its own; give it a matte.
- `solve` picks the start pair with the most parallax among frames whose shared tracks support a pose: the essential matrix and the homography each give candidate poses, and the candidate with the most tracks inside a Sampson error of 1/f pixels and in front of both cameras wins, so a planar yard starts as well as a deep one. A pair counts only when no distinct pose (more than 1 degree of rotation or 10 degrees of travel direction away) holds 0.8 of the winner's tracks: over a short baseline or flat ground several poses fit, and the wrong one folds the scene. Each further frame registers by PnP against the triangulated points and new tracks triangulate once their rays part by enough angle. Every eighth frame a bundle pass moves the 24 most recently placed cameras and the points they see with older cameras held, and at the end bundle adjustment with outlier pruning refines the whole solve. `refine_focal` lets the bundle refine the lens from `yfov`; footage that mostly translates, such as a drone flying straight, leaves the focal length nearly unobservable, and the lens stays near its guess.
- The lens is Brown radial distortion on focal-normalized coordinates: a point (x, y) on the z = 1 plane lands at (x, y)(1 + k1 r^2 + k2 r^4). `SolveOptions::distortion` fixes (k1, k2); `None` solves it. Free distortion trades against depth on footage that has none, so after the pinhole bundle a 20-iteration trial frees k1 and k2 and the solve keeps them only when the rms falls below 0.9 of the pinhole's, then triangulates, prunes and adjusts again with them free. `CameraSolve::distortion` holds the result, [0, 0] for a kept pinhole.
- The world puts the dominant ground plane under frame 0 at y = 0, the origin where frame 0's optical axis meets it, frame 0's view along -Z with +Y up, and scales it so frame 0's camera stands `camera_height` metres above the ground. A `Pose` is a camera position and a glTF rotation (x, y, z, w) looking down its local -Z. `error` is the rms reprojection error in pixels.
- `SolveOptions::pitch` declares a camera that only turns, as on a tripod, with frame 0 tilted that far below the horizon and no roll. Such footage holds no parallax, so it shows no depth or ground, and a wrong focal length makes its turn look like travel; `solve` then skips the reconstruction and finds every frame's rotation with frame 0 the identity, plus the focal length with `refine_focal` (golden-section over half to twice the guess, the rms of the rotation fit as the cost), keeping the given distortion or none. The camera stands at the origin's height `camera_height` above the ground, `points` stays empty, and a pitch from -90 to 90 degrees is required.
- `SolveOptions::zoom` gives that camera a zooming lens: every frame after frame 0 solves its own focal length, each frame's step starting from the one before, and `Pose::yfov` holds each frame's vertical field of view (absent for a fixed lens). Frame 0's lens is `yfov`, or with `refine_focal` the one whose zoom fit has the lowest rms (golden section over half to twice the guess with 100 refinement passes per trial); freeing frame 0 beside the others lets the whole lens drift. A zoom without a pitch fails.
- Footage with too little motion or texture fails with a message that asks for the pitch of a camera that only turns, and so does a missing file, which names its path.
- `light_probe` decodes the footage as RGB through `ffmpeg` at most 960 px wide and stitches up to 64 evenly spaced frames, each through its solved camera, lens and distortion, into a `width` by `width / 2` probe in linear light (sRGB decoded) with Director's environment convention: +Y up, the middle column looking down -Z. Each texel averages the frames that see its direction, weighted by the squared distance to the frame edge up to 0.15 of the frame height, and `coverage` keeps that weight. A pull-push fill gives every unseen texel the colour of the nearest seen ones. Directions sit at infinity, so a travelling camera blurs near things across the probe. An 8-bit plate clips the sun, so the probe holds no sun. `Probe::hdr` writes run-length Radiance scanlines.
- `director-html` calls `solve` at compile time for `<model track>` and stores the cameras in `director_scene::ModelTrack`, and calls `light_probe` for `<model track-light>`.

## Verification

- `cargo test -p director-track --release --test integration`. `klt::` checks sub-pixel tracking on a shifted pattern and refusal on a flat window. `solve::` solves `crates/director-3d/tests/fixtures/solve.mp4` against the true cameras in `solve.json` at the true lens and from a 36 degree guess: reprojection error, camera position and rotation, the image-space error of a grid of points near the origin, and the ground height, plus still footage and a missing file. It also solves `lens.mp4` (the same walk through a k1 -0.15, k2 0.03 lens) at the true lens field of view: the solved bend in pixels at radii out to the corner, the reprojection error and the camera path. `moving.mp4` (the walk past a van, a walker and a rippling pool) solves against the same cameras without and with `moving-matte.mp4`, and a matte of the wrong size fails. `pan.mp4` (the yard from a tripod) solves from frame 0's true pitch at the true lens and from a 36 degree guess, and fails without a pitch or with one out of range. `zoom.mp4` (that tripod zooming from 42 to 24 degrees) solves every frame's lens from a 36 degree guess against `zoom.json`, fits worse with a fixed lens, and fails as a zoom without a pitch. `probe::` stitches `pan.mp4` through the true cameras in `pan.json`: its sky against `sky.hdr`, its view reprojected through frames 10 and 45 against the plate, the fill of unseen directions, the Radiance file decoded back, and missing footage.
