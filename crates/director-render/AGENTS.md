# Purpose

- Paint and export frames of a `director_scene::SceneDocument` and of a live `Director`. This crate is the phase 3 renderer of `docs/plans/10-native-platform-overhaul.md` and the only frame path: core export, preview, stills, tests and the CLI all paint through it.

## Ownership

- `src/lib.rs` owns `Renderer`, `FramePool`, `FrameSession`, `TargetSession`, `Frame` and `RenderError`.
- `src/export.rs` owns `ExportOptions`, `Renderer::export`, `Renderer::export_with`, `Renderer::export_on`, `Renderer::export_options`, `Renderer::frame_times`, `export_director`, `frame_times` and the serial export session over a `TargetSession`.
- `src/passthrough.rs` owns the export over a base video: `Renderer::passthrough_base` (the plan), `Renderer::export_over` (the pool that paints the layers above the video) and the `FrameSession` overlay paint, painted-row scan and band readback.
- `src/frame.rs` owns the frame driver. `FrameState` holds the layout engine and the accumulation and transition rasters across frames; `FrameState::render(director, time, target)` evaluates the `Director` (dirty set, update, layout, post-layout) into a private frame plan of scene layers and transition pairs, paints it through `paint::Painter`, and averages shutter samples for motion blur. The shutter opens at the frame time and spans the shutter angle, so a cut on a frame lands whole. A shutter in which nothing moves paints one sample at the frame time: no transition overlaps it, no enabled clip starts, ends or fades inside it, and no node of an active clip moves at that clip's local times (`paint::subtree_moves`, which asks each node's children over the span `Element::child_time_range` maps its range to). A moving shutter spaces its samples along the furthest travel: `box_placements` maps every node box at both shutter ends (each parent's `Element::child_view_matrix`, such as CSS perspective, wraps its children), `box_travel` measures how far the visible part of each box moves in device px, and `paint_travels` adds `Element::paint_travel` scaled to the device. It paints `ceil(travel / 2.5) + 1` evenly spaced samples, clamped to 2 and the configured count. A transition, a clip start or end inside the shutter, a box point near the screen crossing behind the eye, or a paint travel an element cannot measure takes every sample. `render_frame` paints one frame with a fresh state. `paint_root` paints one subtree on a full pass.
- `src/live.rs` owns the serial live export (`render_export` and `render_export_at_scale`, the parity and bench reference), the `GpuContext` alias (a Skia `DirectContext` under `vulkan`, `()` otherwise), and output scaling (`validate_scale`, `output_dimensions`, `set_output_scale`).
- `src/paint/mod.rs` owns the node traversal (`Painter`: transforms, blend, filter and mask layers, z-index order), static layer reuse and the debug overlays. A node that opens a layer clips the canvas to the device bounds its subtree can reach (`clip_layer`). `src/paint/depth.rs` owns preserve-3d depth sorting and per-pixel occlusion. A cluster of intersecting planes splits through `src/paint/bsp.rs`, which cuts every polygon crossing another's plane and draws the fragments back to front from the eye, each clipped along its cut edges; a cut behind the eye falls back to compositing the cluster per pixel. `src/paint/kinds.rs` owns per-kind knowledge: which kinds and channels vary with time (`node_varies`) or inside a time range (`node_moves`: clocked kinds, audio bindings, transform channels, path motion and `Element::moves_between`), and the kinds this crate paints itself.
- `tests/common/mod.rs` holds the parity helpers: a live `FrameSession` over the original `Director` is the reference for its exported document.
- `tests/rhai_examples.rs` paints every `examples/**/*.rhai` movie (each registered composition with default props) on a live `FrameSession` and from its exported document, and requires both to match.
- `tests/html_examples.rs` owns parity between the document render and the live compiled `Director` for HTML, including `export_director` at half scale with alpha against the live export: every example, a paint fixture, SMIL, looped video, a sampled capture with path and embedded video, text, SVG and nested boxes, `@font-face` faces, and a Paper shader, plus byte equality of `render_frames` and a warm `FramePool` with fresh sessions, and per-frame MD5 equality of a document export with the live `render_export`. Video fixtures need FFmpeg on the path.
- `tests/shared_video.rs` owns decoder sharing: seven elements of one clip at different timings, opacities and visibility start one decode session per playing position on one session and at most one per worker and position across a four-worker export, compiling the page starts none, frames match cold seeks, the four-worker export decodes like the one-worker export, and four workers with the writer decode an audio track once.
- `tests/passthrough.rs` owns the export over a base video: a caption over a full-frame H.264 clip starts no decoder in the engine and shows the layers and source frames of the full paint, a clip with every tenth frame missing shows each frame at its own time on both paths, a frame that paints outside the sampled band exports again over the whole frame, and a scaled, late, RGB, translucent or rounded video, a blend and a backdrop blur over the video all export through the full paint with equal frames, and the base video's own audio track reaches the output as the source's AAC packets, as PCM encoded in step at the source's level, and through the engine mix at half volume.
- `tests/incremental_render.rs` owns incremental frames through `FrameState`: dirty nodes, their ancestors and descendants re-evaluate, clean siblings skip, a clean static subtree rasterizes once, and a full frame updates every node.
- `examples/export_bench.rs` times a 1080p HTML export through the serial live `render_export` against `Renderer::export_with`, plus an encode-only floor (prepainted frames and premixed silence piped through the single-pass encoder), render-only, one-call `render_frames`, single-session and live-session splits. It writes `serial.mp4`, `director-render.mp4` and `encode-only.mp4` to its output folder. `DIRECTOR_BENCH_WORKERS` overrides the pool size.
- `tests/motion_blur.rs` owns adaptive shutter sampling: the shutter opens at the frame time, a still shutter paints once, a clip edge inside the shutter samples every time, a fade takes the shutter ends, and samples space out along the furthest box travel.
- `tests/core_nodes.rs` owns the same parity for core Lottie (with an override image) and audio scenes: the document re-exports unchanged, the mix is sample-equal and frames match, including a spectrum visualizer and an audio binding.

## Local Contracts

- `Renderer` holds an `Arc<SceneDocument>`, the asset loader, an `ImportRegistry` (core kinds by default, `with_registry` for frontend kinds such as `director_html::register_import`) and a render mode (export by default).
- `Renderer::session` imports the document into a fresh `Director` through `director_core::scene_import::import_scene_with` and the renderer's `FrameSources` (`frame_sources`, set by a host through `with_frame_sources`; `export_director_with` passes the exported `Director`'s own), which clones, pools, export workers and the export writer share, so each video file opens one decoder per playing position for all of them. A `FrameSession` owns that `Director`, a `FrameState` and its raster surface, and reuses them across frames.
- `FrameSession::render(time)` and `render_into(time, &mut Frame)` paint through `FrameState::render`, matching the live export for motion blur, transitions, track opacity and clip fades, output scale and incremental dirty frames. Transition and accumulation rasters match the target surface and allocate on first use. It returns RGBA8 at the document output size (`width * output_scale_x`, rounded): straight alpha for a transparent background, and a direct premultiplied read for opaque frames, whose bytes are identical.
- `render_into` reuses the frame's pixel buffer. Use it on every repeated paint: a fresh 8 MB buffer per 1080p frame costs more than painting a static scene.
- `FramePool` holds one lazily imported `FrameSession` per rayon worker and keeps them warm across calls, so each keeps its static layer cache and incremental frame state. `render_into(times, &mut Vec<Frame>)` resizes the frames to `times`, gives each worker one contiguous run, reuses every pixel buffer and returns the first error.
- `TargetSession::new(renderer, context)` imports the document into a render target on a GPU context, or a CPU raster without one. `render_into(time, frame, context)` submits through that context before readback. It stays on the context's thread. The GPU export and `director_host::Session::render_frame` after `use_gpu` paint through it.
- `FrameSession` is `Send` because it exclusively owns its `Director`, layout engine and CPU raster surfaces; a pool drives each session from one worker at a time.
- `Renderer::pool_with(workers)` sizes a pool explicitly. `Renderer::pool_for(frames)` gives one session per `FRAMES_PER_SESSION` (15) frames, capped at half the rayon workers so x264 keeps cores.
- The static layer cache is `Director::static_cache`, so `Director::note_mutation` evicts edited nodes for live edits and each session keeps its cache across frames. A static node rasterizes its subtree with the local transform baked in and reuses it on a full pass while its `StaticCacheKey` matches (layout size, local transform, pivot, inherited opacity, and time when the subtree varies with time). An incremental pass reuses clean layers and never serves a dirty node.
- Compositions paint their nested timeline through `Painter` into `CompositionNode::surface_cache`, using the inner `Director::static_cache`. An element that returns a graph from `Element::sampled_graph` (HTML captures) paints that graph through `Painter` with a fresh cache. Every other kind paints through its own `Element::render`.
- `Director::debug_overlays` draws node outlines and `id z:` labels after every frame.
- `Renderer::export(path, mode)` writes H.264 with AAC (VP9 with Opus for a transparent document) in one FFmpeg process through `director_core::video_wrapper::Encoder::with_audio_input`. FFmpeg settings and export frame times come from the document alone (`Renderer::frame_times`, the document fps, size, transparency and audio sample rate); `tests/common` asserts `Renderer::frame_times` equals `frame_times` of the import for every parity scene. A scoped writer thread spawns FFmpeg, then imports a separate `Director`, mixes the whole track and sends it while a `pool_for` pool paints its first batch, then encodes each batch while the next paints; two frame batches circulate. A failed paint or write calls `Encoder::abort`, so nothing reaches `path`. `export_with` takes an explicit worker count. `export_options(path, options, gpu)` takes `ExportOptions` (encoder mode, draft, workers, a cancel flag checked before each batch, which ends the export with `RenderError::Cancelled` and leaves nothing at the path, and a progress callback called on the writer thread with encoded and total frames) and paints on `gpu` when given. `export_on(path, mode, context)` paints through one session on the calling thread, into a render target of a Vulkan `GpuContext` (flushed before each readback) or a CPU raster for `None`, sending single frames with four in flight; `document_export_decodes_like_the_live_export` checks its raster form against the live export.
- An opaque export without motion blur first looks for a base video (`passthrough_base`): it paints up to 24 frames spread over the timeline as overlay frames (`director_core::passthrough`), and each must clear the surface under the same video file, one source frame further per output frame, and the source's grid rate (`director_core::video_wrapper::grid_rate`) must equal the document's. A variable-rate source on a nominal grid at the document's rate qualifies. The union of the rows those frames paint, padded by a quarter of its height and at least 32 rows, is the band; a band over 60% of the height becomes the whole frame. `export_over` then paints every frame the same way on CPU sessions, as many as keep ahead of 240 frames a second by the sampled paint time and two at least, reads the band with straight alpha and pipes it to an encoder with `EncoderSettings::underlay`, so FFmpeg decodes the video, blends the band over it and encodes. When the only audio is one track of the base video's own file at constant unity volume and center pan, without effects, parameter animations, loop or composition nodes, starting at the source time under the first frame and playing through the last, `base_audio` hands it to FFmpeg as `Underlay::audio`: AAC from source frame 0 copies its packets, anything else FFmpeg encodes, and the writer neither mixes nor sends audio. Other audio, cancel and progress work as in the full export. A frame that paints outside the band ends the attempt and the export runs again over the whole frame; a frame without that base ends it and the export runs through the full paint. `ExportOptions::paint_all` skips the base video. The full paint converts the video to RGB and back, which moves red and blue about 1.5 levels on 1080p footage, and writes full range; the export over a base video keeps the source's samples, range and matrix.
- `export_director(director, registry, path, alpha, scale, draft, gpu)` exports a live `Director` through its scene document and the same pipeline, with the raster scale, transparent background and draft x264 of `render_export_at_scale`. A `gpu` context paints through `export_on`; `None` uses a `pool_for` pool. It checks required fonts, imports with the caller's registry, and restores the output scale and background. The CLI exports every render through it.
- `Renderer::render_frames(times)` paints through a fresh `FramePool` and returns frames in the order of `times`. Warm pool frames equal fresh-session frames byte for byte.
- `FrameSession` and `FramePool` paint on the CPU raster backend. `export_on` and the live export render on a Vulkan target when the caller supplies a `GpuContext` (`vulkan` feature), flushing before each readback.

## Work Guidance

- CPU export is bound by x264: piping frames and the final lookahead drain take about 330 ms of a 3 s 1080p static export. GPU export is bound by the synchronous Vulkan readback, about 3 ms per 1080p frame under x264 load. skia-safe 0.153 exposes no async readback (`AsyncReadResult`), so a faster GPU export needs a smaller readback (GPU YUV conversion) or several contexts.
- `director-core` dev-depends on this crate, so core integration tests paint through it. Core `#[cfg(test)]` unit tests cannot; put frame tests in this crate's `tests/`.
